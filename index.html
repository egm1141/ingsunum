Level 2

Content Creation

Content creation means making videos, streams, posts or images and sharing them with an audience.

  1. 1

    Idea

    Pick a topic you enjoy and others want to see.

  2. 2

    Record

    Capture gameplay, your screen or your voice.

  3. 3

    Edit

    Cut mistakes. Add music, effects and subtitles.

  4. 4

    Publish

    Write a clear title and make an eye-catching thumbnail.

  5. 5

    Improve

    Read the comments, check the views and try again.

Smart creator rules

  • Respect copyright. Use free-to-use music and pictures, or ask for permission.
  • Protect your privacy. Never share your address, school or passwords.
  • Be kind. Write comments you would say face to face.
  • Check the facts. Make sure information is true before you post it.

(Digital content is anything made for a screen. Be a smart creator: respect copyright, protect your privacy, be kind and check the facts.)

Level 4

Coding

Coding means writing instructions that a computer can follow. Every video game starts as code.

Languages

  • Scratch. First steps with colourful blocks.
  • Python. Easy to read and great for beginners.
  • JavaScript. Games and websites in the browser.
  • C#. Used in game engines such as Unity.

bug = a mistake in the code

debug = find and fix the mistake

player = "Alex"
score = 0   # score starts at zero

for coin in range(10):
    score = score + 10   # each coin = 10 points

print(player, "scored", score)

OutputAlex scored 100

(Coding means writing instructions for a computer. Here, ten coins give 100 points. A bug is a mistake, and we debug it.)
